With 40+ programs on the platform, the hardest part is often deciding where to start. Use this as a simple filter.

## Start here if you're new or returning

Choose a **Beginner** program if you are new to structured training, coming back after time off, or you want to rebuild movement quality before chasing load.

No prior experience is required — just willingness to learn the fundamentals properly.

## Intermediate if you already train consistently

Choose **Intermediate** if you have a training base and want better quality: progression with more load and complexity, while still applying Mobility IQ, Movement IQ, and Mindset IQ.

## Advanced when precision is the point

Choose **Advanced** when you already move well and want programming that rewards control and detail. This is not about ego — it assumes strong Movement IQ.

## When in doubt

Start with Foundation (or another beginner program). The principles it builds carry into everything else. You can always switch later — membership includes every program.

## Gym or home?

Programs are designed for gym or home environments. Equipment requirements are listed on each program page inside the app. If you train at home with limited kit, filter for Home programs first.
